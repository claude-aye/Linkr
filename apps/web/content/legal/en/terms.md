1. The client sends a request to a provider, with a description of the need and the preferred dates.
2. The provider accepts or declines the request. Acceptance forms the contract between them.
3. The client pays a **deposit of 20%** of the agreed price, charged by Stripe when the provider accepts the request.
4. Once the service is marked as completed, the client has **72 hours** to confirm completion or report a dispute. The **balance** is then charged to the client's saved payment method.
5. The provider receives the funds in their Stripe Connect account, less Stripe's processing fees and **Linkr's service fee (10% of the agreed price)**.
