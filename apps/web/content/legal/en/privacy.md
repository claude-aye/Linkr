Linkr is currently offered as a **trial version**. No real payments take place: payments run through Stripe's test environment, and most service provider profiles are fictitious. Please do not enter any sensitive information the trial does not need.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what your rights are, in accordance with Québec's *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (Law 25). The French version of this policy prevails in case of discrepancy.

Linkr is a personal project operated by **Ayé Claude BROU, Jr.**, in Québec City, Québec, Canada. Linkr connects clients with service providers.

**Person in charge of the protection of personal information**: Ayé Claude BROU, Jr. Email: [support@linkrapp.com](mailto:support@linkrapp.com)

Any question or request about your personal information can be sent to this address.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only the information Linkr needs to operate.

| Category | Information | Who it concerns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Account** | First name, last name, email address, password (stored only as an irreversible hash), preferred language, province or territory of residence, date of acceptance of the terms, phone number (optional) | Everyone |
| **Provider profile** | Business name, professional title, description, trades and services offered, rates, service radius in kilometres and areas served (zones and neighbourhoods). The exact location of your workshop or home is not displayed | Providers |
| **Verification** | Licence or qualification numbers (e.g. RBQ, CMMTQ, CMEQ, CCQ) and uploaded supporting documents | Providers in regulated trades |
| **Requests and bookings** | Title and description of the need, preferred dates, indicative budget, address where the service is performed and its geographic position, request status, quotes received | Clients and providers |
| **Search** | Geographic coordinates sent during a search (used on the spot to measure proximity, not stored in your profile); searches with no results (anonymous aggregate statistic) | Clients |
| **Payments** | History of deposits and transactions. **No card number** is received or stored by Linkr: card details are entered directly with Stripe | Clients and providers |
| **Technical data** | Server logs (IP address, date, resource requested), for security, troubleshooting and fraud prevention | Everyone |

To get paid, providers submit their identity information (including, where applicable, their social insurance number or business documents) **directly to Stripe**, on its secure pages. Linkr does not receive this information; it only knows whether the account is activated.

## 3. Why we use it

We use your information only to:

- create and manage your account, and authenticate you;
- show providers near you and connect clients with providers;
- verify licences for regulated trades;
- process deposits and balances through Stripe;
- send you the emails the service needs (password reset, request updates);
- keep the platform secure, prevent fraud and fix errors;
- gather feedback on the trial version and improve Linkr.

We do not sell your information, we do not send advertising and we do not use any advertising tracking tools. If we ever want to use your information for another purpose, we will ask for your consent first.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

Linkr uses only what is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. No advertising cookies or audience measurement tools are used.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `linkr_at` | Identifies you during your session | 15 minutes |
| `linkr_rt` | Renews your session without asking for your password again | 7 days |
| `demand-signal…` (session storage, not a cookie) | Avoids counting the same no-result search twice | Until the tab is closed |

The page for adding a payment method loads Stripe's script, which may set its own cookies for payment security and fraud prevention. They are described in [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/en-ca/privacy).

## 6. What other users can see

- **Provider profile**: the business name, title, description, trades, indicative prices, areas served and reviews received are visible to signed-in users. The email address and licence documents are never made public.
- **Reviews**: a review left on a completed service shows the rating, the optional comment, and the author's **first name and last-name initial** (e.g. "Carol R."). The provider may post a reply under the review. If you delete your review, the provider's reply is removed as well. If your account is deleted, the rating and review remain visible for the provider's reputation, but under an anonymous mention ("—").
- **Direct requests**: a direct booking request and the full service address are visible only to the client who made it and the assigned provider.
- **Open requests**: when a request is open to several providers in the same trade, eligible providers see the title, the description of the need, the indicative budget, the preferred dates and an approximate distance, rounded to the kilometre. They see neither your name nor your address. The exact address is shared only with the provider selected.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **During the trial**: your information is kept as long as your account exists.
- **At the end of the trial**: the end will be announced on the site and by email. **All trial data will be deleted no later than 30 days after the end.**
- **At any time, on request**: write to [support@linkrapp.com](mailto:support@linkrapp.com) from your account's email address. We will delete your account and anonymize your personal information within 30 days.

**Geocoding cache.** Addresses entered during a search are kept for up to 30 days (24 hours if the search returns no results) in a cache on our server, with no link to your account. This cache avoids querying the geocoding service on every search.

Stripe retains certain payment data under its own legal obligations, even after your Linkr account is deleted.

## 8. Security and incidents

We protect your information with reasonable measures: encrypted connection (HTTPS), irreversibly hashed passwords, cookies inaccessible to scripts, server access limited to the operator, and regular backups.

If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d'accès à l'information and the affected individuals, and we will keep a register of incidents, as required by law.

## 9. Your rights

At any time, you may:

- **access** the information we hold about you;
- have it **corrected** if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
- obtain a **copy** in a structured, commonly used technological format;
- **withdraw your consent** and request the **deletion** of your account;
- ask for details on how your information is used.

Write to [support@linkrapp.com](mailto:support@linkrapp.com). We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity before processing the request.

If our response does not satisfy you, you may file a complaint with the [Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca).

If we discover that an account was created by a person under 18, we will delete the account without delay and destroy the associated personal information.

**Changes.** If this policy changes significantly, we will inform you by email or on the site before it takes effect and, where necessary, ask you to accept the new version.
